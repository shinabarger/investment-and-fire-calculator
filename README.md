# Compound Interest & FIRE Calculator

This is a single-file, static calculator that projects investment growth and compares it against your FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) number, based on your expected monthly spend and safe withdrawal rate.

## Features
- All inputs default to 0.
- Bar graph stacking starting balance, contributions, and interest earned per year, with a dashed horizontal line marking your FIRE number.
- Pie chart of starting balance vs. contributions vs. interest earned.
- Year-by-year tabular data.
- "Years to FIRE" indicator showing the first year your projected balance crosses your FIRE number.
